## In the beginning, motivation, and impulsive
while I still in my Informatics bachelor degree, at a time I inspired to pursue master degree, after I had my "private tutor" with my lecturer. At that time, that thought just a fleeting, and impulsive side of me speaking at the moment. Not long, the corona virus happens, everything became grinding halt, as a last year students we struggling to finish our bachelor, and with huge free time in our hand. Some started working as intern (WFH), other enjoy last their last year as a student, and some tinkering or experimenting new stuff, I fell in the latter categories. With huge free time, I stumbled a niche topic regarding Quantum Computing, with that I decided to make it as my thesis topics. After corona virus cases started to dwindle and I had to take a internship job in order to graduate, I worked as an IT Support in a reputable automotive company in my hometown. While doing my internship, I continued my thesis research, and one day I found out that Quantum Computing is a really sought after topics for graduate degree. With that my impulsive thought came rushing back, and I became embolden to pursue my master degree.
With that, my hunt for universities started, while working as IT support and finishing my thesis, I done a research which country I will pursue my master degree. From Singapore, Japan, UK, US, EU, China, and Canada, I chose Canada to be my country I will pursue my master degree. After I picked my country, many people ask "Why Canada ?" the answer I gave pretty simple back then, why ? because it's English spoken country, with growing trends where IT graduates are sought after in Canada, and with welcoming culture towards immigrants. With all the questions had been answered, I preparing to go to Canada. After I finishing my bachelor degree thesis, and resigned from my IT Support work (where I had work for 1 year 8 months), I enrolled for IELTS tutor for prepare my English with amazing tutor teacher. With that after I received my bachelor degree diploma certificate, I went to an agent to prepare all my necessary paper works.

---

## Bureaucracy, mistake, and boarding
while meeting a study agent, apparently I have to choose what university, I want to attend with limited knowledge how Canadian graduate school system work, I went to obvious options. Waterloo University, Toronto University, all 10 top university in Ontario province can give, with all universities had been listed I made I grave mistake where I under estimated how complex the all the process and with my limited IELTS score, I can't join all my options. With desperation looming in, and it was already 1 year come and go, I had to lower my expectations since my IELTS score didn't permit I to go my dream campus. With that in mind I decided to enroll to Algoma University, as a graduate student for Computer Science, even though it's not my dream campus, I quite content what had been offered to me. After all administrations finished, I had to face another challenge, where I had to find a boarding since I will travel alone to Canada. With all options I explored, I finally settle for AirBnB service, with that settled, I had to go to capital city to arrange my visitor permit, and student permit. Where according to plan it will be issued 3–4 weeks after my biometrics collected by the VFS officer, suddenly Crowd strike windows error happened, apparently it effected Canadian immigration services, because of that my visa delayed and I had to deffer to next semester which is Winter semester.
With all frustrating administrations had been done, I had to buy my plane ticket in order I go to Canada. So with that I decided 28th December was a good time to go to Canada and start my transitions, with all my luggage packed I flown from my hometown to Canada at 28th December and arrived on Canada at 28th December at the evening. With that all what I have to do was went to my AirBnB house and started my transitions.

---

## Cold, home sick, and reaffirm
With all essential had been unpacked, I went to sleep, the next day I went to explore my neighborhood and tried to learn how the Brampton bus system works. One day without any warning my phone suddenly died, and with that all my connection to my hometown severed. With that, I felt hopeless and tried to find a place which could fix my phone, since my phone is Chinese generic phone, in North America it doesn't have any repair center for my particular brand. With all information, I became more hopeless and decided to buy a new phone, I felt lost when I discover my phone died since all memory from my hometown is in that phone, and finally the home sickness came and hit me like everything was doomed to failure. But I chose not to dwell to much, I believed that if I mourned my misfortune too much I will became depress, so with that in mind I tried to move on. For one week, I tried to cope with my loss and tried to reaffirm what for I come to Canada.
Not long my first week for my Master came, I met some of my classmate where they already started their 1st semester at Fall last year. When I met them, I came to know that not only me have that trouble adjusting to a new foreign country, the cold, the home sickness and everything in between. With that I have to gather my courage to face all the challenge and strafe forward, with that in mind I really look forward what challenge will come forward.
