When randomly browsing Reddit for dank memes, I stumbled a post about quantum computing course by Qubit by Qubit so immediately sign up with zero knowledge in quantum stuff (before I know quantum computation and quantum information, I already interested in particle physics). Little background I'm currently senior years in UPH Medan (Universitas Pelita Harapan) major informatics, before I open Reddit account I usually read Google Discover which I already personalized to my topics which included particle physics, computer hardware, coding and games.

---

Strangely Google know where I want head to it's give me an article about IBM Quantum Experience (which I totally forgot what the article about) so I immediately checks the website, first i think "Wow I found something awesome this is can be my new research field !" (I like to search random computer / computation thing to make an simple experiment) when i take a closer look, then I realize "This not a joke or silly computation it's real deal" after tinkering some gate if I remember I make a circuit using X gate and bunch of Hadamard (it was a random circuit) after that day I just make quantum computation become my new research topics. Several weeks after relentless final projects and homework, I finally start my own research before I focused in quantum computation I focused in x86 assembly, Linux, and Computer Vision.

---

To kill my saturation all day coding I decided to open Reddit account using TOR (In my country Reddit are blocked) and I was greeted with community I like to join, in this time I already watching quantum computers update especially from IBM, Google, D-Wave and IonQ. So with my curiosity I join r/QuantumComputing in there sub-reddit I learned IBM have sponsored Qubit by Qubit to organize quantum computing course which I immediately sign up. I very excited to enroll the course (it's was first time I join like international course) and I learned alot from weekly course which easy to follow and most important it's fun, challenging homework make me try harder to understand quantum mechanic and math (I terrible with number, I just learning from concept) after I comfortable with all quantum stuff (gate matrix, imaginary number, phase, vector, etc) I seek more course or event with quantum computation topics.

---

From same sub-reddit I found out Quantum Hackathon hosted by Stanford and Yale University, which I invite my other friend to join the hackathon. It's turn out challenging with minimal knowledge in quantum computation or quantum mechanic. But with some documentation (we join IBM challenge) and example we make a simple application using Bernstein-Vazirani algorithm for searching a character from a string. In hackathon I meet some amazing people which give me more insight in quantum computation. After I finished hackathon, one of Qiskit representative Junye Huang share IBM Quantum Challenge 2021 I immediately sign up and join Qiskit Slack.

---

In IBM Quantum Challenge 2021 it's contains 5 challenge with different difficulty which I just solve 2 exercises (rest of the exercises are advance topics like using microwave to control qubit, or make simulation using quantum computer to simulate LiH (Lithium Hydrate) molecule) with help other participant I can more understand how CCNOT/Toffoli/CCX gate come from, and how to maximize effectiveness of a circuit. After I finished IBM Quantum Challenge 2021, I become inspire to took IBM Quantum Developer Certification (which I get discount voucher from this medium article).
